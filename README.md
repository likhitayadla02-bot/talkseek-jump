# TalkFind

**Search Recorded Talks. Jump Straight to the Moment.**

TalkFind turns long recorded talks — workshops, lectures, mentor sessions, 
panel discussions — into searchable, timestamped transcripts. Instead of 
scrubbing through a 2-hour video, search for a phrase and jump directly 
to the exact moment it was spoken.

## Problem

Colleges, hackathons, and organizations record hours of valuable content, 
but that knowledge is locked away. Finding one specific moment means 
watching or scrubbing through the entire recording — there's no way to 
search inside a video.

## Solution

**Upload Video → Convert Speech to Text → Store Timestamps → Search → 
Jump to Exact Moment**

TalkFind automatically transcribes uploaded talks, indexes the transcript 
for full-text search, and lets users click any search result to jump the 
video player straight to that timestamp.

## Features

- 🎥 **Video Upload** — upload recorded talks directly, or import via 
  YouTube link/captions
- 📝 **Automatic Transcription** — speech converted to timestamped text
- 🔍 **Full-Text Search** — search any word or phrase across sessions
- ⏱ **Timestamped Results** — see matching text with surrounding context
- ▶️ **One-Click Playback** — jump straight to the exact second in the video
- 📚 **Multi-Session Library** — search across multiple uploaded talks at once
